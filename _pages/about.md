I'm a detail-obsessed technical and scientific editor with over 15 years of writing and editing experience. I excel at helping researchers clean, tighten, and polish documents such as manuscripts for peer-reviewed journals, reports, and grant proposals. I have experience working with non-native English speakers. In short, I'm here to help your document look great for submission! An outside set of eyes can be very helpful for your important works.

I offer two service types: copy editing and developmental editing. Copy editing (or proofreading) is to check documents for basic mistakes, while developmental editing involves carefully reading and processing text to ensure clarity and that it reads well.


<h3>Copy editing: look for basic mistakes</h3>
- missing or repeated words
- correct punctuation and capitalization
- correct spelling

<h3>Developmental editing: does it read well?</h3>
- ensures that the text is written clearly
- wording and phrasing suggestions
- asks questions about anything that could confuse a reader
- reads closely to ensure that the document makes sense in parts and as a whole
- general advice about appropriate topics in document sections, and the organization of topics
- can contribute to reducing text/word count length (what to cut?)
- tap into my full engagement with your document
- includes all copy editing tasks (see above)
- does not include evaluating or making comments about scientific merit

<h3>Rates:</h3>
- copy editing/proofreading: $0.10/word 
- developmental editing: $0.15/word
- subsequent readings: $0.10/word
